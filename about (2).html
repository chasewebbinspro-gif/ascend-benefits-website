Why I Started Ascend

After 15 years working in the health insurance industry, I kept seeing the same thing play out over and over: business owners were overpaying for benefits they didn't fully understand, trusting whatever their traditional broker recommended, and assuming there was no other option.

The truth is, most brokers default to fully-insured, traditional group plans because they're the easiest to sell — not because they're the best fit for the employer. Business owners are busy. They're running companies, managing employees, and chasing growth. They don't have time to dig into the difference between level-funded and self-funded plans, or to understand what ERISA protections actually mean for their business.

That's exactly the gap Ascend was built to fill. I founded this company to be the resource that business owners never had — someone who comes to the table with deep expertise, no carrier bias, and one single goal: help your company save money, improve your benefits, and use those benefits to attract and keep the talent you need to grow.

What We Believe

We believe that every employer with 2 to 250 employees deserves access to the same benefits strategies that Fortune 500 companies have used for decades — not a watered-down version of a plan designed for the carrier's bottom line.

We believe in education over sales pressure. Our job is to give you the information you need to make the best decision for your business — and then help you execute it flawlessly. We don't push a product. We build a strategy.

And we believe that great benefits aren't just a cost center — they're a competitive advantage. When your employees have access to quality, affordable healthcare, they stay longer, perform better, and become advocates for your company. That's the outcome we're here to deliver.

How We Work With You

Ascend operates as a true consulting partner — not a broker who disappears after open enrollment. We take time to understand your business, your workforce, and your goals before we ever make a recommendation. We shop across multiple carriers and top-rated TPAs to find the right fit, and we stay with you through every renewal, every question, and every change your company goes through.

We offer traditional group plans as well — but we specialize in level-funded and self-funded ERISA strategies because that's where the real savings are for growing businesses. Our independence means we can always put your interests first.

What Ascend Stands For

Education First

Most business owners have never been told there's an alternative to traditional group insurance. We lead every engagement with education — giving you the full picture so you can make a truly informed decision, not just the one a broker wants you to make.

Independence & Integrity

We are not tied to any carrier, TPA, or product. Our independence means every recommendation we make is driven entirely by what's best for your business — not by commission structures or preferred carrier relationships.

Real Savings, Real Results

We don't pitch savings — we deliver them. From reducing FICA taxes through preventive care programs to structuring level-funded plans that return surplus to employers, our strategies are built around measurable financial outcomes.

People Over Policies

Behind every benefits plan are real employees and real families. We never lose sight of that. Better benefits mean healthier employees, lower absenteeism, and a workforce that feels valued — and that's good for your business.

Long-Term Partnership

Our relationship with you doesn't end at enrollment. We monitor your plan, support your employees, optimize your strategy at renewal, and grow with your business — year after year, not just at open enrollment season.

Radical Transparency

We show you your claims data. We explain every line of every proposal. We tell you when a traditional plan is actually the right fit — even when it's not our specialty. You deserve the full picture, always.
