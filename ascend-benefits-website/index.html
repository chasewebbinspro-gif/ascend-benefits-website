The Problem

Traditional Group Insurance Is Costing You More Than It Should

Most brokers default to fully-insured, traditionally underwritten group plans because they're simple to sell — not because they're the best option for your business. As a result, employers with 2–250 employees overpay by thousands of dollars per year without ever knowing there's a better way.

Ascend Benefits Consulting Group offers the full spectrum — including traditional group plans — but we specialize in level-funded and self-funded ERISA strategies because that's where growing businesses save the most.
