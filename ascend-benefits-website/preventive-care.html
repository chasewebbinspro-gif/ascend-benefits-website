What Is EHP?

A Stand-Alone Wellness Program That Pays for Itself

$0

EHP (The Employer's Choice) is a Preventive Care Management Program (PCMP) — a comprehensive, stand-alone wellness solution that pairs with any existing group health insurance. It can be implemented at any time, without waiting for open enrollment or a carrier change.

Unlike traditional benefits that cost the employer money, EHP is funded entirely through FICA tax savings generated by routing the wellness program cost through a pre-tax Section 125 Cafeteria Plan.

By encouraging employees to use the Revive Health platform for everyday healthcare needs, you reduce the frequency of claims filed against your major medical plan — helping minimize premium increases at renewal time.

How It Works

Three Components. One Seamless Program.

The wellness program cost is deducted from gross wages on a pre-tax basis. This lowers taxable income, reduces FICA taxes for both parties, and the employee is reimbursed dollar-for-dollar — take-home pay stays identical.

Component 01

PCMP Plan Documents

Specifies all IRC 213(d)-compliant wellness benefits, enabling the pre-tax deduction and reimbursement of the wellness premium.

Component 02

Section 125 Cafeteria Plan

Funded under IRC 106(a), allowing pre-taxing of 213(d)-compliant medical expenses and generating FICA savings for both parties.

Component 03

SIMERP

The Self-Insured Medical Expense Reimbursement Plan reimburses employees dollar-for-dollar based on plan benefits — keeping take-home pay unchanged.
