What Is EHP?

A Stand-Alone Wellness Program That Pays for Itself

EHP (The Employer's Choice) is a Preventive Care Management Program (PCMP) — a comprehensive, stand-alone wellness solution that pairs with any existing group health insurance. It can be implemented at any time, without waiting for open enrollment, a plan anniversary date, or a carrier change.

Unlike traditional benefits that cost the employer money, EHP is funded entirely through FICA tax savings generated by routing the wellness program cost through a pre-tax Section 125 Cafeteria Plan. Employees gain access to a comprehensive wellness platform, and employers keep the savings.

By encouraging employees to use the Revive Health platform for everyday healthcare needs, you reduce the frequency of claims filed against your major medical plan — playing a critical role in minimizing premium increases at renewal time.

How It Works

Three Components. One Seamless Program.

The wellness program cost is deducted from gross wages on a pre-tax basis. This lowers taxable income, reduces FICA taxes for both parties, and the employee is reimbursed dollar-for-dollar — take-home pay stays identical.

Component 01

PCMP Plan Documents

Specifies all IRC 213(d)-compliant wellness benefits. All benefits are compliant, enabling the pre-tax deduction and reimbursement of the wellness premium.

Component 02

Section 125 Cafeteria Plan

Funded under IRC 106(a), allowing pre-taxing of 213(d)-compliant medical expenses and generating FICA savings for both the employer and the employee.

Component 03

SIMERP

The Self-Insured Medical Expense Reimbursement Plan reimburses employees dollar-for-dollar based on plan benefits — not individual claims — keeping take-home pay unchanged.

Who Qualifies

Simple Eligibility Requirements

EHP is designed to be as accessible as possible. Employees do not need to be on the company's group health plan to participate.

01

W-2 Employee

Must be a W-2 employee of the participating company.

02

Major Medical Coverage

Must carry qualifying coverage — employer plan, spouse's plan, individual marketplace policy, or other qualifying insurance.

03

Full-Time Status

Must work 30 or more hours per week. Employer must have a minimum of 10 full-time W-2 employees to qualify.

Employer Minimum: Employers must have a minimum of 10 full-time W-2 employees to qualify for the program.

No company group plan? Employers without a company-sponsored health plan can still offer EHP, provided each participating employee carries their own qualifying major medical coverage.
